# Review work

**Version 1.0.** Follow AGENTS.md and the narrow approved mode. High-risk B/D verdicts use fresh independent native roots. Bounded/policy verdicts use fresh independent sessions with no author history; policy review requires no additional native launcher. Never reuse/fork author history for review. You are already that reviewer; do not launch another role. Assess and hand off; do not fix your review target and approve it. Actual model/effort/calibration status must match packet; green tests never justify weaker oracle reasoning.

## B: blind high-risk test review

Read only approved public contract/interfaces/expectation sources, exact A tests/fixtures and sanitized evidence. No implementation/history/conversations, implementation-bearing state, coverage maps or existing lessons. Before probes arrange source-free warnings/tracebacks/subprocess diagnostics retaining useful messages/locations/status. Disclose exposure; clean reruns cannot restore blindness.

Check scenario/example mapping and no unapproved meaning hidden as data. Independently recalculate numerical oracles; inspect units/profiles/conventions/estimators/tolerances, shared-helper assumptions, parser acceptance/rejection and vacuous checks. Distinguish legitimate alternatives, permitted positive inputs and plausible wrong results. Background references choose no unspecified behavior. Initially passing tests are valid; claimed product-red needs intended valid failure.

Apply the inherited coverage policy from the permitted packet under specification section 5.1. Before acceptance, independently audit requirement/test mapping and plausible high-impact omissions, including relevant reachable failures and permitted inputs; record a short risk disposition. A's authorship alone does not establish adequacy. An accepted checkpoint is the agreed behavioral baseline, not proof of every possible behavior or future branch. Reject speculative or duplicate tests that chase a percentage; missing coverage decisions/new semantics return to intake.

For correction review, inspect prior accepted checkpoint, changed oracle/tests and all test/requirement dependents. Shared parser/observer/fixture/tolerance/schema/expectation changes expand scope. Decide whether unchanged accepted portions retain applicable evidence; author assertions of safety are insufficient. Inadequate mapping/changed approved meaning requires fresh complete affected review. New meaning goes to intake; supplemental tests cannot recreate original test-first evidence. Return exact revised checkpoint/retained evidence and reviewed scope. Count every completed full/delta review cumulatively and preserve prior spending. Diagnose recurring nonacceptances and the concrete cause of each correction; return scoped evidence to the author without a fixed-attempt stop or new approval interview. Actual human-set limits still bind.

Accept, request bounded author correction or classify environment/tooling, test defect, product defect or unresolved requirement. Classification grants no edit authority. Return concise verdict/findings, scope, commands/status/evidence, exposure/limits and cumulative attempts/spending.

## D: high-risk candidate review

Accept review input only after passing canonical full local verification of exact candidate. Inspect approved behavior, test/oracle validity, interfaces/resource/data semantics, coverage/evidence completeness, frozen-file compliance and simplicity. Challenge permitted positive classes beyond green tests, API units/shapes/errors/memory promises and actual native/reader compatibility. Lint proves no semantic accuracy. Form assessment before current-task implementation lessons. No product/test/rule edits. Product findings route to C within named scope after diagnosis; test issues use sanitized A/B correction; meaning goes to intake. Changed evidence inputs renew checks/review. Report acceptance or evidenced actionable findings and limits, with exact identity/evidence and cumulative attempts/spending.

Review the actual implementation for concrete missed approved behavior or high-impact risk beyond the accepted test baseline. Challenge redundant guards/control flow added solely for a percentage while preserving required validation and invariants. Use specification section 5.2 before routing coverage findings: state the missing approved outcome or concrete reachable risk, its evidence and why tests miss it. An uncovered branch alone is not a test defect. Diagnosis/classification may precede readiness review but cannot establish readiness; do not seek acceptance of a known failing candidate.

## Bounded reviewer

The packet names the worker's product/tests/fixtures/infrastructure ownership and established conventional expectations. Independently challenge the oracle against contract, meaningful public-entry tests, behavior, obligation mapping, infrastructure/platform/check preservation, evidence validity and exact passing candidate. Bounded construction is not blind; freshness still separates author/reviewer. Scientific/binary/custom-oracle/data-loss behavior, security/execution-permission changes, substantial interfaces or unresolved meaning/impact require high risk/intake; settled overwrite/truncation repairs remain excluded from bounded work. Do not let maintenance weaken gates or repair unapproved scope. Review changed tests and dependencies, expanding shared-fixture/oracle scope. Default full-local evidence is required; an explicitly eligible CI-authoritative pilot permits preliminary review but final acceptance only after the complete final candidate CI proof under section 6. Never self-repair/approve, accept a known failure or call preliminary review readiness.

Perform the same independent requirement/test and risk audit under specification section 5.1 and record its short disposition. D and bounded acceptance require approved behavior/risk evidence and each selected measured target, with exact metric/scope/report/exclusion/limit checks separately per required platform/package; never union platforms to hide gaps. Unselected metrics are advisory; an unmet selected target blocks. Preserve installed gates until independently reviewed, owner-authorized migration. Under section 5.2, route test gaps to the authorized test owner, unnecessary implementation to its author, new semantics to intake and measurement defects to authorized infrastructure. High-risk corrections retain blind A/fresh B; bounded corrections retain their authorized worker/fresh reviewer. Irreducible target conflicts need an explicit owner policy decision or remain blocked, never a reviewer waiver or repeated unexplained role cycle.

## Policy reviewer / doctor

Policy reviewer assesses a human-requested documentation proposal in a fresh independent session before activation. Check authority, risk boundaries/ownership, blindness, correction scope, cumulative accounting and actual human-set limits, evidence/gate/coverage invariants and cross-document consistency. Read relevant docs narrowly; no product-red suite is required. Findings return to author; reviewer does not repair and approve. Human-requested migration after policy acceptance preserves historical attempts/spending; otherwise in-flight tasks keep their applicable rules. Historical quota records are facts, not renewed caps.

Doctor uses [DOCTOR-PROMPT](../../../docs/agentic-software-delivery-v1.0/DOCTOR-PROMPT.md) in a fresh session. Check worktree and avoid unrelated/active work. Read relevant instructions, lessons and actual evidence, distinguishing instruction gaps from execution errors. --check writes nothing. A confirmed gap permits a small patch only within existing human-requested docs scope; keep spec/AGENTS/templates/prompts/skills aligned. No runtime/tests/workflows/threshold/settings edits. Present diff/evidence; use the existing human request as scope authority and obtain fresh independent policy review before activation. Ask only for missing material decisions, unclear/outside-request objectives or human-required external authority. Commit/push/merge require their actual authority; no duplicate request for an already authorized action. No daemon, speculative thresholds or automatic loop.

Ordinary doctor cannot silently choose an easier coverage policy or activate a relaxation through a failing task's repair. A separately authorized coverage-policy adoption records the explicit owner choice and independently reviewed migration; any executable gate change belongs to the named infrastructure owner. Check cross-document agreement with specification sections 5.1–5.2 and preserve historical accounting and actual human-set limits.
