# Completion cleanup of task scratch

**Version 1.0.** Coordinator workflow for [delivery specification section 9.1](../agentic-software-delivery-v1.0/DELIVERY-SYSTEM-SPEC.md#91-automatic-cleanup-at-task-completion). This document describes a scoped one-shot completion duty; it installs no executable cleanup tool or service. Actual operations keep existing risk classification, named ownership, tests, independent reviews and gates. Data-loss behavior and reusable destructive implementations remain high risk.

1. **Inventory this task.** Before calling it finished, identify its generated fixture repositories and temporary scratch from creation/run evidence. Record exact paths, observed types/identities, task/run association, retained consumers and excluded neighboring material. Roles report only their own process/scratch/reference facts. Do not infer disposability from age, naming, ignored status or chat closure. Do not include predecessor or other-task scratch without its own authority and inventory.
2. **Close and retain.** Close owned writers/processes through their supported shutdown and verify current process/open-handle evidence. Never stop unrelated activity. Retain original required evidence, manifests and archives outside proposed removal units and verify those copies where copying is permitted. Preserve tracked fixtures, active work, real caches/backups, retained artifacts/environments/Python pins and every material check input. Missing evidence or failed/limited activity inspection leaves affected scope blocked; record limits rather than infer inactivity.
3. **Resolve dependencies.** Fingerprint retained regular files and original link texts. Inspect retained incoming links and other known retained consumers, resolve relative/chained targets and required dependencies, and identify ancestors needed at original paths. Original immutable links may require exact in-place referents; moving bytes, archiving them or rewriting links is insufficient. Record discovery boundaries and unresolved consumers. Keep original evidence untouched. Do not traverse symlinks as deletion targets.
4. **Partition and route.** Produce an exact allowlist of positively disposable units and protected units. If a root contains retained referents, plan removal of safe disjoint siblings while preserving referents/ancestors at required original paths. If the partition or dependency proof is uncertain, preserve affected units and report why. Apply the existing risk route and required tests/reviews to the exact operation and any helper; this workflow grants no maintenance exemption. Immediately before mutation, recheck path/type/identity, protected dependencies and owned-process inactivity; changed or missing proof stops that scope. Use only the reviewed scoped operation, with no recursive symlink-target traversal or automatic retries. A new reusable destructive tool requires its own authorized high-risk contract and route.
5. **Verify the result.** Confirm actual removed units are absent and protected units remain at required paths. Compare retained regular-file hashes, original link texts, resolution/dependencies and protected cache/environment/Python pins with before evidence. Confirm relevant tracked state and material verification inputs remain valid. Diagnose failures, preserve remaining material and route correction within ownership; renew affected proof if inputs changed. Obtain the independent review required by the operation's route. A list of hashes alone cannot verify retained links.
6. **Record completion honestly.** Link one local receipt from the sole Current state. Include inventory/operation/candidate identities, times, commands/status, process checks, before/after proof and review; exact removed/trimmed/preserved/blocked scope/reasons; defined counts and native allocated-byte measurement/unit/conversion; actual limits or not set, unknowns and next action. Distinguish planned from observed removal. Shared-filesystem free-space deltas include concurrent activity and cannot establish task-attributed reclamation. State only the inspected custody/verification scope. Verified preservation exceptions may yield zero removal; unchecked/blocked scope prevents an unqualified cleanup-complete claim and passes to the next coordinator. Retain required evidence and useful lessons before disposing of scratch.

No product-red tests or full product run are invented merely for this documentation workflow. Existing exact-candidate checks and required cleanup-route evidence remain binding. Never delete material inputs to avoid a failed check or make an old receipt appear current.
