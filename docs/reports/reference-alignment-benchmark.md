# RA01: fixed benchmark and comparison contract

**Version 1.0. 28 October 2026. Report-only intake.**

The initial benchmark is the existing stationary, ordinary single-objective linear three-dimensional structured illumination microscopy (SIM) synthetic case: three orientations and five specimen exposures per orientation. The [versioned manifest](../benchmarks/reference-alignment-v1.json) freezes its actual inputs, settings, provenance and unavailable owner data. The [authorized roadmap](../plans/reference-alignment-roadmap.md) orders RA01–RA15. This report runs no fit or expensive study, adds no numerical runtime, and certifies no repaired reconstruction or C/Python parity.

## Four separate claims

| Claim | Evidence needed before claiming success | Present disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Algorithm/method alignment | Match imaging map, basis, constraints, noise metric, penalty, supported modes and convergence; derive permissible coordinate changes independently | Proposed controls in RA02–RA13; library and study implement different estimators |
| Numerical stored-input agreement | Exact paired arrays/settings, deterministic declared conversions and independently justified arithmetic/discretization budgets | Not established for C; matched tiny Python controls can proceed without C data |
| Physical recovery | Independent truth or held-out informative measurements, observable-spectrum/source-basis analysis and predeclared resolution controls against a matched conventional baseline | PR53 remains poor recovery: 95.3% source and 66.4% noiseless-signal prediction error; resolution improvement unproven |
| Owner-output compatibility | Owner raw/calibration data, metadata, exact options and paired C outputs, followed by fixed convention conversions | Required paired owner bundle absent from the inspected evidence; RA15 blocked on that bundle and resolved mode/acceptance choices |

Successful execution, a finer grid, a smaller training residual and an optimizer success flag satisfy none of the latter three claims by themselves. C source is a comparator, not an unquestionable oracle. The [PR54 investigation](pr53-recovery-investigation.md) gives only orientation 0/phase 0 known-source errors: 1.6988% at true illumination, 45.6367% at frozen estimated illumination, 66.3078% for the original fitted prediction on that denominator. They are neither the full15 aggregate, additive causal shares, a lower bound nor a parity result.

## Modes, actual settings and open choices

The selected synthetic mode preserves all 27 specimen objects, three calibration beads, independent actual exposure phases, unequal beam amplitudes, clear matched-index scalar pupil, immutable seeds and supplied geometry. Linear means linear fluorescence/imaging in the source; the observation noise still includes photon shot noise and Gaussian read noise. The small eight-object case is a separate fixed control, not a substitute or a second realization of the full case. No owner instrument mode is inferred from these synthetic settings.

Full detector Z/Y/X is `(32,256,256)` at `(0.125,0.08,0.08)` µm. Its field is `(4,20.48,20.48)` µm: `32×0.125=4`, `256×0.08=20.48`. Fine generator source is `(64,512,512)` at `(0.0625,0.04,0.04)` µm; the inverse basis is `(32,512,512)` at `(0.125,0.04,0.04)` µm. Pairing fine axial cell masses preserves mass, not position or predicted images. Calibration detector is `(8,32,32)` at the same detector spacing, with nonzero origin `(0.125,-0.375,0.23)` µm. Plans preserve every catalogue row, phase, carrier and origin; manifest hashes bind them rather than rounded prose.

Generator camera relation is `counts = 10 + 1.7*(Poisson(mu_e+6)+Normal(0,2.5²))`. Dark excludes the background. Read standard deviation is `1.7×2.5=4.25` counts; true flat-minus-blank contrast is `1.7×1000=1700` counts. Inference instead uses estimated blank `20.1973062` counts and contrast `1700.0256947` counts for the full case. `(counts−blank)/contrast` is reference-flat-relative signal, not electrons/photons. Excitation/emission wavelengths are 0.488/0.525 µm, refractive index 1.515, detection numerical aperture 1.4, pupil quadrature order 256 and 2×2 pixel midpoint samples. Calibration source refinement is 8×8×8; specimen refinement is 2×2×2. These finite settings certify no continuum convergence.

## Convention dictionary

| Item | Frozen interpretation and deterministic comparison rule |
|---|---|
| Axes/storage/order | Physical arrays Z/Y/X; carriers Y/X; generated bundle orientation/phase/Z/Y/X. Signed volume order labels are 0,±1,±2. C uses X/Y carrier structs, X half-spectrum/padded real rows and acquisition section ordering. Explicit permutation/storage completion precedes arithmetic; C's single `dxy` does not represent unequal Python lateral sampling |
| Length/frequency | µm for coordinates/wavelengths; `nm/1000=µm`. `k_rad_per_um=2π*q_cycles_per_um`. Integer bin `b=q*N*spacing`; sign is retained, no nearest-bin rounding of a physical carrier |
| Fundamental/second harmonic | q is the fundamental, order m has lateral m*q and phase m*φ. C configured line spacing can describe the highest lateral harmonic and divides by `nordersIn−1` in the inspected ordinary 3D path. Resolve metadata before a factor-of-two conclusion; order-two optical transfer is not generally order-one squared |
| Phase/sign | `I_p=dc+2 Re(c1 exp(iφ_p)+c2 exp(2iφ_p))`; cosine/sine `(B,C)` maps to `c=(B−iC)/2`. A common φ→φ+δ requires c_m→c_m exp(−imδ). Independent phase errors are not one common gauge. C cosine+i·sine storage must be mapped together with its assembly ramp/origin/scale, not conjugated in isolation |
| Origin/gauge | Direct sites are origin+(index+1/2)*spacing. Shift phase from calibration origin to specimen origin once: φ_spec=φ_cal+2π*q·(origin_spec−origin_cal)_yx. Do not reapply this to already-transferred PR54 records. Permitted global side exchange/conjugation is a declared gauge, not fitted registration |
| Conjugation/reflection | Real spatial exposures have c_-m=conj(c_m); frequency negative bands obey D_-m(k)=conj(D_m(−k)) on all axes. Modular even-Nyquist reflection and explicit physical ±endpoints differ. Complete half-spectrum only under declared symmetry; complex empirical orders cannot be replaced by magnitudes |
| Fourier normalization | Python volume public spectra use normalized forward DFT and unnormalized inverse. Kernel preparation has separate mass convention; direct cache uses its explicitly unnormalized forward/normalized inverse representation. Trace phase/direction counts, pixel area, transfer normalization and output grid once. Constant/displaced-point controls must agree without fitted brightness scaling |
| Pixel integration | Direct detector midpoint mean is multiplied by detector area; the inverse's four fine sites carry `dy*dx` each, summing to detector area. Measured empirical OTF may already include detector response. Never add a second pixel factor by analogy |
| Source-cell mass | Source entries are integrated emission weights, not density. No source voxel-volume or focus-spacing multiplier. Evaluation reference is `(true_gain/estimated_contrast)*(fine[2k]+fine[2k+1])`; it is a coarse mass reference, not a continuum or prediction-equivalent source |
| Axial sign/model boundary | [Periodic synthetic model](../models/synthetic-3d-acquisition-v1.md) defines Δz=focus−source; [finite optics](../contracts/simulation-optics-v1.md) and [inverse](../contracts/simulation-inverse-operator-v1.md) define d=source−focus. The central field has a positive axial exponent in both written conventions. For identical unequal side labels this reverses first-order axial factors; balanced sides can conceal it. These are separate contracted models. Derive a full sign/beam-label/transfer mapping before equivalence; do not silently edit either contract. Effective kernels require profiles fixed relative to focus |
| Boundary/support/crop | Native periodic equality and exterior-zero finite equality are different questions. Full-volume/all15 metrics are primary for the study. Each future matched control predeclares one physical support and integer crop/voxel coordinates; no error-selected support, alignment or fitted scale. Stored source and detector planes can differ because their spacings differ |

## Comparisons and tolerance derivation before acceptance runs

For estimate a and reference b on the same declared grid/units, report signed `a−b`, absolute Euclidean error, reference norm, relative error `||a−b||₂/||b||₂`, maximum absolute error, and element count. If the reference norm is zero, relative error is unavailable; retain absolute metrics. Preserve complex components, not magnitude alone. Full prediction aggregates concatenate every exposure with one reference denominator; per-exposure ratios are supplemental and cannot be averaged into that aggregate. Keep source coefficient, noiseless signal, corrected mean and raw training residual comparisons separate. Use the corrected stable evaluator; original evaluations remain historical records.

Matched RA02 controls independently assemble finite discrete Fourier transform (DFT) sums and a real-coordinate observation matrix. Check synthesis, adjoint inner product, covariance/multiplicity, normal-equation residual, objective and observable subspace. Choose tiny well-conditioned full-rank cases and explicit rank-deficient/unsupported cases. Arithmetic tolerances must be derived by the blind test author from precision, operation/reduction scale, phase-design conditioning, independent reference error and the public represented-input promises, then independently reviewed before running acceptance. Do not fit a tolerance to the candidate's discrepancy or demand bitwise platform agreement.

For the study, `J=||Ax−Y||²/(2M)+λ||x||²/(2N)`. Multiplying by M yields normal penalty `μ=λ*M/N`. Here `M=15*32*256*256`, `N=32*512*512`, so `M/N=15/4`, and `λ=0.001` gives `μ=0.00375` before Fourier/noise conversions. This is a units sanity check, not an adopted C Wiener setting. Complete matched covariance, regularizer, basis, constraints and convergence are needed for equality; positivity-active or finite-stopped estimates need not equal a linear spectral inverse.

Physical discretization tolerances require independently measured source/pixel/pupil/boundary refinement errors; numerical agreement budgets cannot stand in for recovery accuracy. Statistical comparisons must predeclare the observation/noise model, uncertainty construction, independent repeats/held-out observations and multiplicity treatment. The current single fixed noisy realization supplies no measured statistical tolerance. RA14 physical recovery and RA15 owner compatibility criteria remain unresolved decisions to settle from informative evidence **before their acceptance runs**. RA01 deliberately adopts no numerical recovery percentage, universal resolution threshold or automatic Wiener rule. If missing informative observations make identification unavailable, report that outcome rather than invent a premise.
