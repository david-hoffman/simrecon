## Representation implications and safe public ordering

I=2^31-1, M=2^63-1. Native half-volume is <= native full-volume for even X>=2;
the separate retained half bound is therefore implied by the full bound under
this schema, although actual half array extents and aggregate axes still bind.
Output-plane<=output-volume because Zo>=2; signed element checks retain both
named meanings without demanding unreachable isolated failure witnesses.
Each historical byte expression is <=8I (or4I), hence <M whenever its retained
signed count holds. This does not preserve its obsolete unsigned/signed32 byte
cap. Since Q<=P and Q*P<=I, Q²<=I, odd Q<=46339, O<=23170 and8O<=185360; the
old order-byte cap is already redundant. Actual f64 accumulator extent dominates
f32 final extent for equal shape; both widths remain explicit obligations.

The integer-only `_extent` calls cover inclusive M, M-1 and exact above-bound
products at widths1/4/8/16, aggregate axes and old-byte-cap crossings. They create
no target arrays. Separately, a genuine new declaration with native(2,1,2),
L=2^14/A1 has output(2,16384,32768), plane2^29 and volume2^30. Historical output
workspace bytes2^33 and lookup bytes2^31 cross the old byte caps while signed
counts/native bytes remain valid. Only tiny quadratures/noise are copied;
consumer Wiener0 must reject statically before any enlarged allocation/FFT.
This observes actual public admission/order for those two crossings. Tiny
invalid geometry tests with inconsistent backing prove rejection/category only;
they do not isolate which competing shape/count guard fired. `_extent` plus
tiny calls does not prove every real caller path; D must audit the paths and
absence of obsolete guards. No huge success or motivating geometry is executed.

Finite c64 real inverse/products have magnitude <=f32 maximum. With D*O<=I,
all signed accumulation magnitudes are bounded conservatively by2*I*f32max,
well below2^161 and binary64 overflow2^1024. Thus S32.15 has no reachable isolated
f64-accumulator-overflow requirement after its earlier finite guards. Finite
required w<=f32max also gives w²<2^256, so overflow of the required f64 square
is unreachable; square underflow-to-zero remains reachable and tested. Positive
scale computed from signed geometry has denominator no larger than2^155 under
the loose individual bounds, hence remains positive finite in f64; forged
zero/nonfinite/false consumer scales are still tested. Coordinates are bounded
by about2^159 and phase products by about2^321 under declared counts, pitches,
carrier/modulation magnitudes; this supplies no universal libm accuracy proof.
Derived spacing can overflow at tiny positive pitch and is separately rejected.
