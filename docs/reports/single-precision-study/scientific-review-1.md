## Scientific reasoning and first-slice sufficiency

The reference method is independently meaningful. The sparse inverse sum uses
the CE04 positive inverse sign, no normalization beyond the declared upstream
alpha, signed side factor two and centered positive carrier ramp. Unit-transfer
denominators include self first, eligible signed others in their prescribed
order, strict other support and Wiener square last. The rational eighth-root
enclosure is valid: independently squaring its endpoints brackets 2. Its width
is reference uncertainty; it is not candidate error. No production transform or
filter helper supplies these reference values.

For the frozen dyadic band declarations the representation term is exactly
zero. The empirical-style `1+2^-30` example supplies a separate nonzero consumed
conversion term. The report does not call double/single agreement scientific
truth. Its absolute lower/upper errors retain near-zero and negative values;
RMS is honestly a rounded descriptive statistic, and the component-sum
conditioning quantity is a conservative upper measure for complex witnesses.
Physical recovery under noise/model mismatch is outside the accepted claim.

I independently reconstructed all 127 corrected scalar rows from their case,
time, direction, order and frequency declarations, without importing study or
product helpers. Every exact scale and reported scale-component error matched
the CE04 rational signed-order formula. This validates that reference family;
it does not make the supplemental candidate scalar code a future runtime.
The original kernel's exact-derived weights and reference-derived opposite
snapshot remain important limitations. The separate corrected full-component
observer is useful evidence but does not retroactively turn the original runs
into full filtering/recombination measurements.

The acceptance method provides a usable independently justified starting point
for #113. For the DC case, three unit powers plus `w^2=1/4` give denominator
`13/4`, scale `4/13`, and correlated rounded assembly
`s32+2*(-s32/2)=0`. For the near-cancellation case I independently obtained
exact reference `1/3407872`, condition `2097151`, candidate
`2.980232238769531e-7`, and absolute error `4.584972675030048e-9`.
The stated a priori local-bin bound is `4.470346942619938e-8` before any
nonzero FFT term and final conversion. It covers this observation without
being fitted to it. Both half-ulp scale bounds and the side-product half-ulp
are valid in the stated bins. This is a computational rounding bound, not a
physical accuracy target.

The scalar interval construction, component product bounds, signed accumulation
and explicit final rounding are sound methods once an actual operation sequence
is frozen. Relative gamma bounds apply only under their stated finite-normal
assumptions; the absolute subnormal contributions and overflow disposition are
necessary. The supplemental `gamma(32n,u64)` is only a conservative diagnostic;
it must not replace reviewed interval evaluation of the eventual sequence.
For a constant-transform witness the mathematical transform is trivial, but a
future candidate still has to demonstrate the prescribed transform semantics
and exactness for that witness. This review supplies no blanket zero FFT term.

Its A/B checkpoint must establish a fixture-specific justified transform

For carriers/modulation and discrete decisions, interval comparison is the
right method, including exact analytic ties and the strict-else/first-maximum

discarded diagnostic fields needing invented preparation history or raw replay.

Output dtype cannot establish FFT internals, and f64 accumulation plus f32 output
