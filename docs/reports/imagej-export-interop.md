Resource cases exercised 16 and 384 planes at 512×512 uint16 with a 128 MiB opaque extension and block bounds 1 and 3. Accepted Python/NumPy allocation assertions passed: peak below 64 MiB and increase below 32 MiB as source pixels increased from 8 to 192 MiB. These are tested bounds, not exact recorded peak measurements or constant resident-memory claims. Source mmap auditing passed; the implementation uses no mmap and streams one selected plane at a time. Opaque hashing uses 1 MiB chunks. TIFF directories and OME metadata grow with planes/series. Viewer RAM, throughput and latency remain unmeasured.

Exclusive-destination, pre-access source-identity and real completion-change cases passed. The operation-time file-size-limit case returned ordinary EFBIG after a diagnosed byte-write correction. A later failure can leave a partial new file. Identity checks are not an atomic snapshot and do not detect every same-size/same-timestamp payload edit. No automatic deletion, atomic publication or crash-durability guarantee is made.
