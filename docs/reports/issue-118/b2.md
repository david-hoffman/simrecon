### Current-camera/first-off capture truth

The T2 fixture's on raw arrays are exact integers. There is one normal-loader
sentinel at t0/d0/z0/p0. Its rolled off values are positive or -.25/-.5, never
-1. Normal loading therefore changes corner background5 to its neighbor3.5
and slope.75 to1 exactly once; the prepass itself only repairs its local raw
corner and does not mutate maps. Time0 sees the original map and time1 the
changed map. The between estimator ignores normal slopes/extra offsets/alpha,
uses phase average divided by X*Y*Z, only the first off exposure, and fixed10
edges on the phase sum. Z2 gives subz1 and first_z0.

A stale time1 map differs by the fixed10 image of a corner impulse
`2*(5-3.5)/(Z*Y*X)=3/240`. The test constructs that field before the public
call and requires a margin over the independent center errors. It separately
requires nonzero centers, distinct times, a substantial second-off field and
unique correlation maxima with margins over twice the propagated error. The
actual erroneous second-off accumulation would also subtract its background;
the large positive patterned second-off mean remains a discriminator after
that subtraction (the isolated rolled sentinel does not cancel it).

I traced `between_expected -> edge/transform -> correlate/correlation_field ->
fractional_cross/full/peak`, including Disc construction, indexing, real/absolute
projection, reductions, contractions, transcendental/division error algebra,
unique peak, curvature, offset range and strict half-wrap checks. The symbolic
correlation shortcut is ineligible here because highpass=.2. The actual ordered
build/correlation events are checked in both times, and numerical centers,
Fourier arrays, cross spectra, real/absolute correlations, neighbors, offsets,
