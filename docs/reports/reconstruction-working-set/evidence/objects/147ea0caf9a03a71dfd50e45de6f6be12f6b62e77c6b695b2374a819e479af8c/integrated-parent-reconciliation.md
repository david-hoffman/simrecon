## #115 integer domain and original target

For required positive allocation bytes, exact Python-integer products must fit
native intp (`2**63-1` on the supported 64-bit platform); old byte-only
signed32/unsigned32 caps are superseded. Signed32 dimensions/element counts,
native/padded/half/output products, loop/index/cutoff/offset rules, aggregate
native strides/extents and actual transfer stencils remain. Representation and
backend/allocation success are separate. There is no general product RAM cap.

The motivating `(T,D,P,Z,Y,X)=(1,3,5,32,2048,2048)`, O3/L2/A1 has native volume
134,217,728, padded real count 134,348,800, half-volume 67,174,400, output plane
16,777,216 and output volume 536,870,912. These geometry counts fit the retained
signed limits; actual complex128/native/image extents fit native addressing.
The old 4,294,967,296-byte output expression is therefore no longer a geometry
refusal. Other declarations/stencils/branches still require valid inputs.

No target array or workflow was allocated. For a hypothetical float32 caller at
that geometry, source arithmetic alone gives caller raw 8,053,063,680 B and an
independent same-dtype workflow snapshot of equal size. Prepared q has
`16*T*D*(2*O-1)*Z*Y*(X//2+1)=16,121,856,000 B`. All three coexist during CE06
preparation: **32,227,983,360 B** before frame/FFT/off/metadata/native overhead.
This is a source-calculated co-live lower bound, not measured RSS. It already
exceeds the preliminary exposed 17,179,869,184-byte bound. Thus representation
validity does not admit allocation, and the target remains unmeasured/outside
this finite corpus. A binary64 caller would be larger still; the #115 contract's
15 GiB raw example has that different caller dtype. No 16 GiB workstation claim.

## Small exposed-domain method delta

The preliminary coordinator record at
`/workspace/work/campaign/evidence/116/resource-domain-preliminary.md` reports
outer/Ubuntu visible membership `0::/`, exposed finite limit 17,179,869,184 B,
private Docker cgroup namespace, no separately requested Docker memory limit
and no active swap at that historical observation. Its readings are asynchronous
and **not** actual-time admission evidence. Unknown physical-host ancestors
outside the exposed machine remain unknown; root visibility does not prove
absence or unlimited memory.

The obsolete `hidden_ancestors_resolved` grant flag is rejected. Its replacement
is `resource_domain` with scope `WS116-exposed-domain-conditional-v1`:

- `external_ancestors: "unknown_unobservable"` and nonempty
  `conditional_rationale`: explicit residual risk, applicable smaller evidence,
  competing work, no swap rescue, and why a conservative attempt is justified.
- `correspondence_evidence`: durable actual-time proof `path`, `sha256`, positive
  `observed_at_unix_ns` and a finite positive `maximum_age_seconds` selected by
  the coordinator. The file hash and age must match. Clock/evidence trust limits
  remain; this is coordinator evidence, not an engineered security boundary.
- `expected_exposed`: exact membership, mount_root, mount, cgroup `namespace`
  link identity, and `visible_memory_max` path-to-native-limit map, reconciled
  against actual observer facts. The durable proof must explain outer/container/
  workload correspondence; equal root-looking paths alone are insufficient.
- Positive integer `predicted_child_peak_bytes`, `native_allocator_allowance_bytes`
  and `reserve_bytes`. The prediction is a conservative source/smaller-measurement
  envelope covering the whole child/caller/observation scope, not an observed new
  peak. Native allowance and caller/OS/observer/competing-activity reserve need
  explicit evidence/rationale; there is no default numeric choice.

Admission requires at least one finite observable bound; every visible ancestor
has a usable current/limit and explicit stop threshold. Prediction plus native
allowance must fit the process stop. At each visible ancestor, current plus
process stop plus reserve must fit its stop threshold, which itself remains
strictly below any finite limit. Host MemAvailable and all earlier per-attempt
representation, monitor, ownership and cancellation protections remain binding.
The child complete record must match the admitted exposed namespace/limit map.
Unknown external ancestors remain disclosed conditional risk rather than
invented certainty. Changes, missing correspondence/bounds or unresolved ownership
block admission. Sampling and all safety decisions remain fallible; no zero-OOM,
aggregate-tree peak, process-ceiling product API or workstation certification.
