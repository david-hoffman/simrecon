## Invariants, risks and exclusions

Keep stable original prepared coefficients while writing a nonoverlapping filtered destination. Preserve literal Y traversal (including odd-Y omitted-row/copied-value behavior), cutoff writes/zeros, m=0 handling, negative partner reflection over **all axes**, conjugation and even/odd `(p+n)/2` and `(p-n)/(2j)` reconstruction. Reused scratch must be reset for every required index/order/direction/time; stale cutoff/outside-domain data can silently corrupt results. Do not cache scratch across public calls or alter exception/finite checks, destination publication, dtype or thread behavior.

Full mode's original/outer/helper-return q backings are distinct from this scratch; deleting one source early would change numerical dependencies. Do not present a three-q-copy saving as a result of the plus lifetime change. Native2D, zero/positive-order distinctions, odd/unit dimensions, failed finite writes and caller/result ownership require affected public checks.
