# HP114 numerical-acceptance proposal

**Proposal: do not activate a product precision mode on this evidence.** No image,
parameter, phase, runtime or memory threshold is inferred from a favorable maximum
or fitted to make the six rows pass. The owner selected no tolerance/latency ratio/
RAM cap, and independent full-grid/upstream uncertainty and physical-usefulness
premises remain unresolved. Contract§4 permits noactivation where meaning cannot
be justified. This is the proposed numerical decision, not a missing metric filled
with a guessed tolerance.

The complete149 observations justify the following bounded evidence policy:

1. **Storage representation:** accept only explicit declared real binary16 or
   real/imaginary paired little-endian storage with verified nearest-even words,
   exact promoted component bits including signed zero, original units and full
   loss/overflow/subnormal accounting. This exact codec correspondence was observed;
   it is not scientific accuracy acceptance or a public complex32 dtype.
2. **Finite behavior:** preserve every signed image, warning, nonfinite conversion,
   undefined zero-reference/zero-phase metric, exception category and ordered
   decision. A finite candidate does not automatically pass. A nonfinite fixture
   intentionally witnessing overflow is retained as such, not converted into a
   success or a global half failure. No clipping/scaling/fitted cutoff is allowed.
3. **Relative-to-wider assessment:** retain actual count-unit absolute/RMS and
   dimensionless relative errors, parameter cycles/um/bin and amplitude/phase
   effects, all three repetitions and frozen/replay metadata scope. The measured
   range includes H1 W05 max0.1310071264566659 counts and W06 relativeL2
   .019978077714797954; these observations are not proposed accuracy thresholds.
4. **Activation:** no profile qualifies merely through unchanged observed discrete
   projections or small relative errors. Scientific acceptance for a named future
   use requires independent reference/error uncertainty and physical-use meaning,
   with all permitted decisions/failures and unsupported envelope defined before
   implementation tests. No scalar/vector-libm, FFT internal precision or full-grid
   premise may be silently assumed. This study leaves those premises unresolved.
5. **Resources:** only W02/W05 prepared matched results support their exact lifecycle
   comparison. They show no observed terminal-peak reduction and overlapping timing
   ranges, with setup/pack/unpack/caller/result retention included. No16GiB promise,
   isolated allocation peak, general speed threshold or unmeasured-site negative.

This proposal distinguishes conversion correctness, relative deviations,
independent scientific validity, physical utility and activation. It does not demand
zero error from half experiments or impose a new condition/residual cutoff. A
future activation proposal must return to a separately bounded high-risk contract
and fresh required reviews; final SCI independently evaluates this noactivation
proposal against the exact completed report.
