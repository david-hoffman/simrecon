R2. Native searched 3D uses coarse/refinement pair (0,2). Rebuild at refinement
entry, then use that *same frozen spatial pair* for all angle probes, all
magnitude probes and the final selected order-2 fit. Native true 2D uses (0,1),

and its search closures *before* later-order overlap builds. Use a clear
ownership/scope design that passes normal static/scope checks; a pure one-line
deletion of a closure-captured variable is insufficient. In 3D, later fits are
ascending (0,b), b=1..O-1 except 2, at the final carrier. In searched 2D, later
fits are adjacent (b-1,b), b=2..O-1. Preserve exact build/fit counts and order.
The 2D O=2 no-later-order case still completes correctly. Known/reused branches
retain their (0,b) direct fits and objective=None without a synthetic refinement.

R4. Preserve all fitted/raw/final factors, 2D cumulative products, floors,
phase policy, time-zero carrier reuse and fresh later-time modulation fits.
Search-all still starts each time at supplied initial vectors. Compact outputs
retain all ordered descriptors and scalar decisions; equal-valued repeated
builds remain distinct indices and every trace reference stays valid. Full
results retain every complete immutable overlap Fourier/spatial/write-mask
record and correlation/trace record, even for repeated values. Captures cannot
be reconstructed by replaying numerical work.

R5. Preserve caller independence: accepted writable, read-only and strided
inputs remain supported and unchanged; results retain owned/read-only arrays
and accepted stage mutability. Caller buffers may stay live throughout the call
and may change after return without changing results. Compact results must not
retain the discarded pair through a hidden record, array base, closure or other
reachable reference. Full records remain complete and independent.

R6. Complete structural validation, actual-stencil checks (including zero-weight
vertices), representation limits and finite diagnostics still run in the same
scientific stage/order. This includes discarded reverse/combined/correlation
and search objective calculations. Preserve direct ParameterEstimationError,
workflow category/stage/context/cause rules, MemoryError and interruption, and
whole-call failure with no partial result or mutation. Preserve existing actual
error precedence; do not invent a precedence for simultaneous invalid inputs
where the public contracts leave it unspecified. No success fallback, silent
flag disable, epsilon exclusion, new signal floor or new domain cap.
