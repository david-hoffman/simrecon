# Workflow examples

**Version 1.0** These illustrate policy; they are not executed results or product approvals.

## Mechanical complete request

Request: “Fix the three broken documentation links to the existing public contract.” The targets and non-normative scope are settled. Intake records that actual instruction, mechanical route, named files, relevant link/diff checks and inherited budget, then sends a concise read-back. It does not ask for another yes. One worker makes the deterministic edit and checks it; owner acceptance suffices unless the contract asked for review. Full exact-candidate local verification still precedes any PR submission; successful link checks alone do not waive it.

Changing a coverage threshold or delivery approval rule in the same file changes policy/gates. That is outside mechanical scope and needs its separate approved independently reviewed route.

## Bounded conventional repair and infrastructure

An approved conventional command prints a supplied list of ordinary names to standard output in the same order, but reverses a two-name input. The public behavior is established and no scientific, binary, data-loss, permission or ambiguous interface meaning changes. Intake records bounded scope and explicit ownership of the command plus named regression tests. One worker inspects implementation, independently justifies the expected outcome, writes meaningful public-entry tests and repairs. A fresh reviewer challenges that oracle, permitted inputs, actual behavior and exact passing candidate. This route openly loses blind test construction; uncertainty promotes it to high risk.

Authorized lint/dependency/platform maintenance also uses a bounded worker with named workflow/config/test ownership and a fresh reviewer checking preserved platform/check semantics. It cannot remove coverage obligations for convenience. A failure revealing a new execution-permission boundary needs high risk rather than another maintenance attempt. Default worker allowance is one corrective cycle after initial work unless otherwise approved; later sessions and splits preserve spending.

## High-risk interface and numerical contract

“Export my customers as CSV” has material access-control/data scope. Intake inventories ownership/fields/escaping/errors, reuses architecture and obtains approval of the identified contracts. High risk applies to uncertain interface and access-control meaning. Fresh blind A tests real user/public entry points, fresh B independently reviews expectations, C implements against frozen tests, and full local verification passes before PR submission. Full CI and fresh D assess the final candidate; merge remains the owner's action.

Existing approved behavior may pass initially. Do not mutate product or invent red; C may confirm no product edit is needed. A claimed bug requires a valid test reaching and failing for the approved reason. Broken database setup is environment/tooling, malformed CSV observer is a test defect, a valid export failure is product defect, and missing field intent is unresolved requirement. These classifications route repairs but do not lower risk or grant file ownership.

For a numerical API, unspecified intercept units return to intake. An approved scale-ratio invariant can support a narrower claim while the absolute interpretation stays unresolved. A derives tolerances from approved math/precision, not output; B independently checks legitimate variation and plausible wrong values. A three-line endian, unit or settled overwrite/truncation repair remains high risk despite size. Conventional expected behavior does not qualify data-loss risk for bounded work. Green tests and 100% coverage cannot establish omitted allowed inputs.

## Scenarios and coverage examples

One approved decision says the command accepts a non-empty sequence of ordinary names and preserves their order. Three lengths may be equivalent coverage examples under that scenario. A new rule rejecting an empty sequence, interpreting a name as a path, or promising stable output on disk-write failure is a distinct decision/outcome and another scenario. Branch count, parameterized test count and data volume do not determine scope. Five scenarios is the default; approve a larger coherent contract once rather than hide unrelated cases in a row.

## Shared-oracle correction and author continuation

B previously accepted a profile-aware metadata oracle. D later supplies a public reproduction showing the expectation wrongly assumed one unit convention. Coordinator classifies test defect, sanitizes the finding into approved profile/input/expected outcome and excludes implementation/conversation/coverage clues. A may continue its own blind author session if unexposed, or use a fresh permitted author. It supplies changed tests and dependencies derived only from tests/requirements. A shared profile fixture expands review to every dependent case.

Fresh B decides delta versus complete affected review, whether retained accepted evidence remains valid, and exact revised checkpoint. C resumes only after that acceptance. Added tests are supplemental if product already existed; acceptance cannot recreate test-first history. The correction has a recorded window/reason and retains all earlier rounds, budget and used C repairs. Two nonacceptances trigger diagnosis before another rewrite; model escalation is no reset. B/D always remain fresh independent verdict sessions.

## Environment evidence, waits and model calibration

Before expensive roles/checks, verify current interpreter/platform/lock/install/import/isolated environment health. Reuse a valid observed reviewer launch; do not start another reviewer to probe connectivity. Supported bounded waits yield until completion/failure/action, with a total deadline within budget. Timeout is incomplete evidence and consumes allowance, never a successful review or silent retry.

A receipt binds candidate/tree/tests/fixtures/lock/check/base/environment/platform, material ignored inputs, exact native coverage and artifact/version inputs. A changed environment invalidates affected evidence despite unchanged HEAD. Git-derived versions can differ between PR head/integration/merge even with equal trees. Valid D evidence need not repeat a full successful run; default submission still needs full verification for the exact changed candidate.

Choose models by oracle/acceptance complexity, not code lines. Calibrate public/synthetic known-defect and allowed-positive cases under recorded criteria, comparing complete accepted-task cost, valid oracles, missed/false findings and rework. Cheaper/medium options stay candidates until that evidence supports the named family. A/B/D scientific or custom-oracle work starts conservatively; a small conventional pilot cannot establish its quality. Keep route/model/gate/hotspot comparisons distinguishable and unknown metering explicit.

## Conditional CI-authoritative evaluation

A settled bounded task may evaluate section 6's alternative only after separate approval and effective native protection/trusted complete expected check/base/artifact proof. A protection endpoint returning no legacy configuration does not prove no ruleset exists; inspect actual effective controls. A green required name can conceal skipped/missing results without a complete-set aggregate. Unknown prerequisites keep full local gating. High risk and changes to the alternative's own gate/proof are initially excluded.

An eligible task passes focused local checks before submission, then full final-candidate CI and fresh independent acceptance before owner merge. It is unverified while awaiting CI. Known failures still block submission; newly discovered CI failure stops readiness and consumes normal repair budget. No untested final artifact or release approval follows from tree equivalence or merge. Profile local duplicate-run/hotspot cost before optional caching/sharding; no helper is mandatory.

## Policy and doctor

Doctor distinguishes an evidenced instruction gap from execution error under an existing readiness rule. --check reports without writing. A confirmed gap produces a scoped patch to existing spec/AGENTS/templates/skills, with actual approval of the identified proposal and a fresh independent policy review before activation. Existing owner authorization needs no duplicate yes. Runtime/test/workflow repairs remain separate scoped work; no fabricated product-red suite is needed for policy docs.

Owner-authorized migration applies only to named work and preserves historical charges. Keep one Current state and concise evidence-linked handoff, with scenarios/examples, windows/rounds/repairs/budget, per-role model responses/tokens, elapsed/owner-wait/check time, defects/exposure and unknowns. Overlapping checks/review are not additive elapsed time; all runner/model costs still count. Root LESSONS entries remain unique append-only files; Version 1.0 and Git preserve revision history.
